Services

From day-to-day bookkeeping to major decisions, and representation before the tax courts.

Tax advice
Direct taxes and IVA (Italian VAT), tax planning, dealings with the Agenzia delle Entrate (the Italian Revenue Agency) for businesses and individuals.
Accounting and financial statements
Ordinary and simplified bookkeeping, annual financial statements and explanatory notes.
Corporate advice
Company formation, choice of legal form, articles of association, shareholders' meetings, extraordinary transactions.
Business planning
Budgets, business plans and business valuations, so that decisions are made with the numbers in front of you.
Contracts
Shareholders' agreements, agreements with directors, customers and suppliers.
Employment and staff
Staff management and labour costs, together with trusted consulenti del lavoro (Italian payroll and employment advisers).
Tax disputes
Tax notices and assessments: review of the notice, settlement with the tax office, appeals and representation before the Corte di giustizia tributaria (the Italian tax court).

Frequently asked questions

Can't find yours? Ask me.

Do you also work with clients outside Asti?

Yes. With video calls, shared documents and digital signatures we can work well remotely: many clients come to the office rarely or never.

At what time of year can I start?

At any time. At the first meeting we look together at where to start and which documents are needed.

How do I send you my documents?

However suits you best: email, WhatsApp, a shared folder or in person at the office. You don't need to send electronic invoices: with your authorisation I download them directly from the Agenzia delle Entrate (the Italian Revenue Agency).

How much does it cost?

As a starting reference I use the up-to-date fee schedule of the Associazione Nazionale Commercialisti (ANC), adapted to your case: tax regime, number of documents, services required. After the first meeting you receive a written quote with the fee agreed in advance.

Do you also prepare income tax returns for private individuals?

Yes: modello 730 and Redditi persone fisiche (the two Italian personal income tax returns), with deductions, property, rental income and, when needed, inheritance tax returns.

How quickly do you reply?

Within 24 hours on working days, by phone, email or WhatsApp. For urgent matters close to a deadline, it is best to call.

In which languages can we talk?

Meetings are held in Italian or English. Documents and correspondence can also be in English.

I live abroad and own a home in Italy. Can you help me?

Yes: codice fiscale (Italian tax code), IMU (the Italian municipal property tax) on the home, rental income and the Italian income tax return for non-residents. The work is done remotely, in English.
