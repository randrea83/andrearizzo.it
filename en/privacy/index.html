Privacy notice

How the personal data of visitors to andrearizzo.it and thefiscallab.it, and of anyone who contacts the office, are processed.

This is an English translation provided for convenience. In case of doubt, the Italian version prevails.

This notice is provided pursuant to Article 13 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR). It applies to this website and to the blog The Fiscal Lab (thefiscallab.it).

Who the controller is

Andrea Rizzo, Dottore Commercialista (Italian qualified accountant and tax adviser), Corso Dante 193, 14100 Asti, Italy. Partita IVA (Italian VAT number) 01715340053. Email infohttps://www.andrearizzo.it/.it, telephone +39 0141 594693.

No data protection officer (DPO) has been appointed: one is not mandatory for this activity.

What data

Browsing data. The website uses no analytics, advertising or profiling. The server hosting the website automatically records some technical data about visits: IP address, date and time, page requested, type of browser.

Data you send us. If you send an email, phone or send a WhatsApp message, we process the data you give us: name, contact details, content of the message. When you first get in touch, please do not send documents or data that are not needed, in particular data concerning health.

Calculators. The data you enter in the calculators stay in your browser and do not reach the office. You can find the details on the Cookies page.

Why, and on what legal basis

  • Answering your questions. Basis: our legitimate interest in replying to those who write to us (Art. 6.1.f GDPR).
  • Setting up an appointment or preparing a quote that you request. Basis: pre-contractual measures taken at your request (Art. 6.1.b GDPR).
  • Running the website and protecting it from misuse and attacks. Basis: our legitimate interest in the security of the website (Art. 6.1.f GDPR).
  • Defending a right, if a dispute arises. Basis: legitimate interest (Art. 6.1.f GDPR).

We do not use your data for advertising or profiling. We do not take automated decisions about you.

If you become a client, you receive a specific privacy notice together with the engagement.

Do you have to give us your data?

No. But without contact details we cannot reply to you. Browsing data are necessary for the website to work.

Who receives the data

  • Website hosting. GitHub Inc. (United States), which publishes the website through its GitHub Pages service. GitHub records visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes and processes them under its own privacy statement.
  • Email. UAB Hostinger (Lithuania, European Union), which processes the data on our behalf as a processor.
  • WhatsApp. If you write to us on WhatsApp, the service is run by WhatsApp Ireland Limited, which processes the data as an independent controller under its own terms.
  • Authorities. Only where required by law.

The data are not sold or passed on to others.

Data outside the European Union

Browsing data of the website are transferred to the United States, to GitHub. GitHub states that it complies with the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and also uses standard contractual clauses; under the Data Privacy Framework the transfer is based on the European Commission’s adequacy decision of 10 July 2023 (Art. 45 GDPR). Email stays with Hostinger, in the European Union. To find out which safeguards any transfer is based on and to obtain a copy of them, write to infohttps://www.andrearizzo.it/.it.

How long

  • Questions and quotes that do not lead to an engagement: deleted within 12 months of the last contact.
  • If a dispute arises: kept until it is concluded.
  • Browsing data: kept by GitHub for the periods stated in its privacy statement; the office has no access to them.
  • If you become a client: the periods stated in the privacy notice for clients apply.

Your rights

You can request at any time:

  • access to your data and a copy of them;
  • the correction of inaccurate data;
  • erasure;
  • restriction of processing;
  • portability, where the processing is based on a contract or on pre-contractual measures.

Right to object. Where we process data on the basis of legitimate interest, you can object at any time on grounds relating to your particular situation (Art. 21 GDPR).

To exercise your rights, write to infohttps://www.andrearizzo.it/.it. We reply within one month.

You can also lodge a complaint with the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali, the Italian data protection authority (garanteprivacy.it).

Changes
