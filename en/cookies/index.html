Cookies

The website does not use profiling, analytics or third-party cookies.

This is an English translation provided for convenience. In case of doubt, the Italian version prevails.

This website does not set profiling, analytics or third-party cookies and does not use tracking tools. That is why there is no cookie banner: under the Guidelines of the Garante (the Italian data protection authority) of 10 June 2021, consent is only required for cookies and tools that are not technical.

Browser storage

The flat-rate or ordinary regime calculator saves the values you enter in your browser’s storage (local storage), so that you find them again if you come back to the page. This is a technical tool, used only for the function you are using. The data stay on your device and are not sent to the office or to anyone else. You can delete them whenever you like from your browser settings, under the option for clearing site data.

External resources

None. The fonts and the graphics of the calculators are hosted on the website itself: opening a page does not send data to other services.

Links to other websites

The website contains links to LinkedIn, Facebook, WhatsApp, Google Maps, the Garante’s website and the blog The Fiscal Lab. None of these services is embedded in the pages: you only come into contact with them if you open the link. From that moment, their own cookie and privacy rules apply.
