About me

Andrea Rizzo, Dottore Commercialista (Italian qualified accountant and tax adviser) in Asti. Background, experience and way of working.

My name is Andrea Rizzo and I am a Dottore Commercialista, the Italian professional title for qualified accountants and tax advisers. I have been working in accounting, tax and business advice for over ten years. I hold a degree in Business Administration and Accounting Professions and have been a member of the Order of Dottori Commercialisti and Esperti Contabili of Asti since 2022.

The office is at Corso Dante 193, in Asti. I work with clients in the city and the surrounding province, and also in other regions: much of the work is done remotely, with shared documents, video calls and digital signatures.

I hold consultations in Italian or English.

Who I work with

  • Small and medium-sized businesses: bookkeeping, financial statements, tax returns, tax planning.
  • Self-employed professionals: choice of tax regime, the flat-rate regime (regime forfettario), bookkeeping and social security contributions.
  • Start-ups: company formation, business plans, incentives and funding.
  • Private individuals: income tax returns, inheritance, property.
  • Amateur sports associations and companies (ASD/SSD): formation, tax regime, sports work.
  • Non-profit organisations (Enti del Terzo settore): RUNTS (the national register of non-profit organisations), financial statements and reports, 5 per mille (the share of income tax that taxpayers can allocate to non-profits), tax relief.

How I work

I use technology to cut out unnecessary work, for me and for my clients: documents collected in an orderly way, deadlines kept under control with dedicated tools, quick replies. When a task can be automated, I automate it, so that time is left for the thinking.

For matters outside my field I work with a network of trusted professionals: payroll and employment advisers, lawyers, expert appraisers, financial advisers and internationalisation experts. The client has a single point of contact and a complete answer.

Before we start, you receive a written quote listing the services included and the costs.

I write on The Fiscal Lab

On The Fiscal Lab I publish articles on tax, companies, sport and the non-profit sector: new rules explained in practical terms, with references to the legislation. The articles are in Italian.
